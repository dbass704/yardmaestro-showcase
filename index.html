Landscaping software · Built in North Carolina

YardMaestro

Two connected apps for the landscaping trade. Homeowners photograph their yard and get realistic redesigns with plant lists and price ranges. Landscapers run the business side: photo quotes priced from their own price list, measuring, routes, crews and plants.

Built by a lawn-care owner who managed crews at two landscaping companies Stack TypeScript · React · React Native · Fastify · Postgres Tests 850+ automated

For homeowners: see your yard redesigned

A phone app. Snap the area, answer a few questions, and get three design options with plants that grow in your zone.

For landscapers: run the business

A web app for the office and a crew app for the field, built around how lawn and landscape crews actually work.
